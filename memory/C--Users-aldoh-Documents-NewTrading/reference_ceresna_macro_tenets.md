Ceresna restates five tenets at the start of each Macro Outlook (verbatim in `Transcripts/macro_outlook_20261005_transcript.txt` 00:09-00:44). macro_context records them as reading principles in `RStudies/reports/macro_context/template.html`, in scanner order (Ceresna's number in brackets):

1. [C1] Understand the present to evaluate possible scenarios. → 00, 09.
2. [C3] Markets diverge from fundamental value for long periods but revert over a long enough horizon. → WARNING only: macro_context serves BOT (2-4 wk), so valuation is no reason to fade a breakout or exit early; reversion inside the horizon shows up as a liquidity turn (principle 3).
3. [C4] Over short to intermediate horizons prices follow liquidity, not value, in BOTH directions. Tightening (dollar, long yields, MOVE, credit: 02/07/09) hits small caps / equal weight / breadth first, remaining money crowds into a few leaders, then everything sold together. Loosening broadens participation (EW and Russell outperform, breadth widens).
4. [C5] When everyone is in consensus, something different almost always happens. → COT 11, positioning stress 07.
5. [C2] Markets are made of investors, traders and bankers, not currencies, commodities, stocks and bonds. → 11, COT by trader group.

**Why:** until 2026-10-06 the template had 4 principles: C3+C4 merged and liquidity narrowed to "liquidity stress" only. Narrow leadership is NOT an ample-liquidity sign; the Daily Bias already scores weak breadth as Liquidity Stress.

**How to apply:** when comparing Ceresna transcripts with macro_context, map content onto these five. Exclude his advisory messages from scanner content: "play defense", hedge tenor, collars, overweight/underweight calls, buy-on-dip. Proposal list in `Transcripts/wherestrade_20261091_summary.md`.
