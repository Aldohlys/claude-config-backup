The three facts that drive any future discussion of the name:

1. Biosimilars are 114% of growth. H1 2026: biosimilars 33% of sales at +20% cc added ~USD 299m at
   constant currency while generics (-1% cc) gave back ~USD 37m, against ~USD 262m of group cc
   growth. Group growth is a biosimilar launch-cadence question, nothing else.
2. European generics is the funding block, not a growth asset — USD 2.08bn, 36% of group sales,
   0% cc. It pays for the Slovenia build; it is never the bull case.
3. The 2030 plan is already in the price. At ~15x EV/FY26E core EBITDA and ~21x core EPS the
   spin-off value gap has closed. Company targets: core EBITDA margin 24-26% by 2028, 25-27% by
   2030, >30% by 2035 with net sales more than doubled (Bio100, >100 biosimilars by 2040). Today's
   EV is ~10x the 2030 target EBITDA — the market pays for delivery, not for the option on it.

(Owozy with Adalvo, July 2026), tirzepatide autoinjector ANDAs accepted by FDA June 2026. Guidance

April 2026 executive order mandates a generics review within a year, and 100% duties on imported
generics from 2028 have been floated. North America is ~24% of sales; tariffs are explicitly
excluded from guidance.
