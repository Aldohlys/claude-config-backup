As a European (Swiss-resident, CHF base) investor, the user buys Irish UCITS funds/ETFs rather than the US version of the same strategy (stated 2026-09-22, re Neuberger EMD Hard Currency: IE00B99K4563 vs NEMD).

**Why:** US-domiciled funds carry US estate-tax exposure for non-US persons, US withholding on distributions, and often no KID/ICTax-friendly reporting; Irish UCITS pay no withholding on distributions.

**How to apply:** when analysing a fund or ETF, find the Irish (or Luxembourg) UCITS share class and compare it against UCITS alternatives (e.g. VEMT/IEMB for EM bonds), not against US ETFs like EMB/VWOB — use US ETFs only as performance proxies, and say so.
