Galderma (SIX: GALD) is a covered name as of 2026-09-22. Reports:
`Trades/GALD_vs_SDZ_comparison_20260922.md` and the Word version

Sandoz side comes from [[project_sandoz_coverage]].

Three facts that drive any future discussion of Galderma:

1. Growth is one drug. Nemluvio (nemolizumab) did USD 433m in H1 2026 from nothing two years ago,
   guided above USD 1bn for 2026, peak-sales estimate doubled to above USD 4bn. Strip it out and
   group growth falls from 24.6% cc to roughly 9%. The base — injectable aesthetics, 46% of sales —
   grew 12.1% with fillers soft, in a quarter when AbbVie's aesthetics line grew 0.3%. Discretionary,
   consumer-paid, cyclical.
2. The valuation carries the return. ~29x FY26E EV/EBITDA and ~42x core EPS against ~20% cc growth.
   Mid-term guidance (updated Mar 2026) is a 15-17% cc sales CAGR 2023-2027 and +450-550bp margin
   vs 2023 (2023 base: USD 4.08bn, 23.1%). Even on delivery that is ~23x 2027 and ~19x 2029. The
   multiple, not the operations, holds most of the distribution both ways.
3. The dated risk is Q1 2027. Relfydess (relabotulinumtoxinA) took a US CRL on 1 Jul 2026 —
   manufacturing-site observations and analytical methods, not efficacy or safety; approved in 33
   other markets. Management deferred mid-term guidance beyond 2027 to Q1 2027 pending the FDA
   discussion. That event sets 2028-2030 expectations while the stock prices continued high-teens
   growth. Do not buy ahead of it without accepting that.

Register cleaned up: EQT exited in full via a CHF 4.9bn block on 13 Mar 2026; L'Oréal holds 20%
after buying a second 10% from the consortium. The PE overhang that capped the name in 2024-25 is
gone; L'Oréal at 20% is an anchor and a deterrent to any other bidder.

Comparison verdict for a 2-3 year hold: Sandoz is the core (needs delivery, not a re-rating — hold
15x into 2028 is ~11%/yr plus a rising dividend); Galderma is the smaller, later, event-timed leg.
Technicals agreed at the time of writing — GALD below a declining 50/100-day with an unbroken
lower-high sequence from the 185.80 June ATH; SDZ above all four MAs on a compressed 67.9-68.6 base.

Both entered the SMI on 21 Sep 2026 — see [[reference_index_rebalance_volume_signature]] before
reading late-September price action in either name as fundamental. Both report in USD and list in
CHF, so for a CHF book each adds the same translation factor on ~29% local vol — a reason not to
size both at full weight.
