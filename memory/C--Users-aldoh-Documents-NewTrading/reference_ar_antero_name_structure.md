Durable structure of AR, established 2026-08-31. Quarterly figures decay — these three facts do not.

**It is not a pure gas name.** ~4.1 Bcfe/d, of which roughly 70% dry gas and 30% liquids: ~121k Bbl/d C3+ NGLs, ~87k Bbl/d ethane, ~8k Bbl/d oil. That C3+ count makes AR the **largest US exporter of propane and butane** — about a third of revenue tracks seaborne LPG economics, not Henry Hub. When modelling AR, a gas-price-only thesis is wrong by a third. Watch the C3+ premium/discount to index and the ethane premium as separate drivers.

**The hedge book routinely decouples realised price from spot.** Q2 2026: field realisation \$2.66/Mcf, blended realisation after derivatives \$3.90/Mcfe. Coverage was ~2/3 of H2-2026 gas volume and ~40% of 2027. Consequence for any directional trade: **a gas rally does not reach the P&L at full beta in the hedged period** — AR's real leverage is to the forward strip beyond the coverage, not to front-month gas. Always pull the current hedge table before assuming AR is a gas-beta vehicle.

**Single-basin Appalachian since the HG Energy deal.** Dec-2025 announcement, closed Q1/Q2 2026: bought HG Energy II's core WV Marcellus for \$2.8bn, sold the entire Ohio Utica for \$800m. Added ~385k net acres offsetting the existing footprint and ~5 years of core inventory. So AR is now a concentrated Marcellus operator — no Utica diversification, and net debt roughly doubled to fund it.

**Trading character:** beta to SPY ~0.34 with a *negative* 60-day correlation (−0.23 as of 2026-08-31); 60d correlation +0.91 to RRC, +0.82 to EQT, +0.67 to XLE, +0.40 to NG=F. Genuinely diversifying against an equity book, but fully redundant against any other Appalachian gas name.
