- L'Oréal, Air Liquide (also Engie, Hermès, …) pay +10% dividend on shares registered (nominatif pur
  or administré) for 2 full calendar years; cap 0.5% of capital per holder. Nominatif administré
  keeps the shares at the broker — a form at the bank, not a custody move.
- Eligibility shows in the ISIN. Only the main code trades; registered shares are moved to loyalty
  codes by vintage year.
  - L'Oréal: trading FR0000120321; eligible FR0011149590; vintages e.g. FR001400UH43 (registered
    2024 → premium 2027).
  - Air Liquide: trading FR0000120073; eligible FR0000053951 (+ vintage codes).
  - A holding older than 2 years still under the trading ISIN = not registered.
- NO premium: TotalEnergies (confirmed), GTT (none found).
